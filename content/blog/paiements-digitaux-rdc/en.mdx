Digital payment is no longer only a technical channel. For a business collecting payments in the DRC, it supports traceability, availability and customer trust.

## Start with the customer journey

Before adding a payment solution, the business should clarify when the customer pays, which method they use and which confirmation they receive after the transaction. This keeps the integration useful beyond the technical implementation.

## Keep payment flows visible

A reliable payment setup should help operations teams answer three direct questions:

- was the payment started correctly?
- is the transaction status available?
- can the reconciliation data be used by the business team?

These checks matter more as volumes grow or as several payment methods are offered in the same journey.

## Make the integration maintainable

The integration should stay readable for technical teams: clear documentation, a test environment, reliable callbacks and explicit error messages. The easier the flow is to diagnose, the easier it is to reduce interruptions for merchants.
